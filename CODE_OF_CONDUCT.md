# Code of conduct

OpenSource Radar is for people making their first open-source contributions, so we want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone starts somewhere.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities and opinions.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, or using the project to advertise unrelated things

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers through the email address on their GitHub profiles. Reports are kept private. Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues, or block people, to keep this a welcoming place.

This code of conduct is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
