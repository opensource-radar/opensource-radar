Notable changes to OpenSource Radar, newest first. Each entry links to its pull request.

## 2026-11-09

- `/api/health` for uptime monitors ([#47](https://github.com/opensource-radar/opensource-radar/pull/47))
- A short demo GIF in the README ([#46](https://github.com/opensource-radar/opensource-radar/pull/46))

- Clearer messages when the AI helper is busy or switched off ([#44](https://github.com/opensource-radar/opensource-radar/pull/44))
